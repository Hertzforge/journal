The data behind every figure, one file per figure. Published under CC BY 4.0.

| File | Figure | One row is |
|---|---|---|
| `fig1_measured_frequency_change.csv` | 1 | one measured case: its group (joint condition), the structure and mode, the excitation, the lowest and highest frequency change from low level to the highest level tested (percent; negative is softening; equal values for a single number), whether the number is stated in the source's text or read off a figure's axis, the source, and where in the source |
| `fig2_timeline.csv` | 2 | one published item: the year it first appeared (online, presented or filed), its kind, its label on the figure, how much of it was read (read in full, read in part, abstract only, record only), what it is, and its citation |

Numbers read off a figure's axis are approximate and given to no more than two significant figures.
Every value comes from the source named in its row; the article's reference list gives each source in
full.
