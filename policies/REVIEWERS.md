# Guide for Reviewers

Reviews are public, signed and published under CC BY 4.0. Review the article as published.

## Before You Start

Declare any conflict. If you work where public reviewing is restricted, tell the editors: a
review can be published with your name withheld, and the page will say it was withheld at your
request.

## What to Look At

- Whether the evidence supports each claim.
- Whether the method is described well enough to reproduce.
- Whether limits and failures are stated.
- Whether prior work is credited.
- Whether the figures and data say what the text says.

If you can, rerun the code bundle and say what you found.

## How to End

There is no accept or reject. End with an assessment of how well the evidence supports the
claims. Be direct and specific. Criticize the work, never the person.

## The Template

Use `templates/REVIEW.md`.

## After You Post

The authors answer in the same thread, revise as a new version, or both. Your review is linked
from the article and archived with it, so it outlives the discussion.
