# Policies

How Hertzforge Journal publishes, reviews, corrects and keeps its articles.

## Open Access and License

Every article is free to read. Text, figures, data and reviews are published under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 license (CC BY 4.0); code is published under the MIT license. Anyone may
reuse an article, with credit to its authors. Authors keep their copyright. There are no fees to
publish or to read.

## Authorship and Credit

Authors are the people who did the work. Co-authored articles state who did what, using the
CRediT roles. The sources of ideas are acknowledged by name. AI tools are not authors. When a
method from an article is built into the Hertzforge software, the software and the
acknowledgments page name its originator and link the article.

Authors are listed by name and ORCID iD. Articles claim no affiliation.

## Publication Before Review

An article is published once the editors have checked that it is in scope, complete, properly
licensed and reproducible as submitted. The editors do not judge whether it is right; review does
that, in public.

## Public Review

The editors invite reviewers for every article, at least one from outside the author list.
Reviews are signed and published beside the article. A reviewer's name may be withheld at the
reviewer's request; the editors know who wrote it. Anyone may comment. Each article shows its
review status.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Review open | Published. Invited reviewers are named as they accept; reviews and comments are welcome |
| Reviewed | At least one invited review is posted and the authors have answered it or revised. Review stays open afterward |
| Retracted | Kept visible and marked, with the reason. The DOI stays |

## Versions, Corrections and Retraction

Every version is kept and never changed. Corrections and revisions are new versions, each with a
note of what changed. An article found to be seriously wrong is marked retracted, with the
reason, and stays visible.

The one exception to "never changed" is a fix to presentation that changes no text, data or
figure, such as a broken link. It may be applied to a published version and is logged on the page.

## How the Work Was Made

Every article states the tools used to produce it, AI tools included, and what they were used
for. Authors are responsible for every word, number and line of code.

## Conflicts of Interest

Authors and reviewers declare conflicts. When an editor is an author, the other editor handles
the review; when both are, the article page says so and reviewers are drawn from outside the
author list.

## Data and Code

Every article publishes the data behind its figures and the code that draws them.

## Evidence

Every article states whether its results come from simulation, a bench or real test hardware.
An article that argues a position or maps published work states what it rests on instead.

## Use

Methods are published for research. Anyone who applies a method to a test is responsible for
protecting the equipment and the article under test.

## Archiving

Every version is archived on Zenodo with a DOI, independent of the journal's website.
